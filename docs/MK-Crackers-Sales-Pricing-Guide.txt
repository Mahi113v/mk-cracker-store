8. LEGAL AND BUSINESS NOTES  (please confirm with a CA / lawyer)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Firecracker sale is licensed and state rules differ. Your customers (shop owners) are
     responsible for their licences and delivery rules. Add a clear disclaimer in your agreement:
     your software only manages enquiries, stock and orders.
   - Payments go straight to the customer's own UPI id; the money does not pass through you.
     Keep it that way (it avoids payment-aggregator rules).
   - If you cross the GST threshold, charge 18% GST on software service and give invoices.
   - Sign a simple agreement: who owns the data (the company), backup duty, support hours, price changes.
